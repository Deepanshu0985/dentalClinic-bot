# Dental emergencies

## Same-day emergency appointments
We keep time free every weekday for dental emergencies such as severe toothache, a broken or knocked-out tooth, a lost filling or crown, swelling, or a broken denture. Emergency exam with X-ray: $129 self-pay (free with the membership plan once a year). Call (512) 555-0147 as early as possible in the day.

## Knocked-out adult tooth
Time matters: the best results are within 30–60 minutes. Pick the tooth up by the crown (the white part), not the root. If it is dirty, rinse it gently with milk or water without scrubbing. If possible, place it back in the socket and bite on a clean cloth, or keep it in a cup of milk. Call us immediately at (512) 555-0147.

## Outside opening hours
If you have a dental emergency outside our opening hours, call (512) 555-0147 and follow the message to reach our on-call dentist.

## When to go to the hospital
Go to the nearest emergency room or call 911 if you have swelling that affects breathing or swallowing, swelling spreading to the eye or neck, a high fever with facial swelling, heavy bleeding that does not stop after 15 minutes of firm pressure, or an injury to the jaw or head.
