# Services and prices

All prices are typical self-pay prices in US dollars. The exact price depends on your situation and is confirmed by the dentist after an examination. Insurance can reduce your cost.

## New patient exam and cleaning
New patient visit (comprehensive exam, full X-rays and a regular cleaning): $199 self-pay. It takes about 60–75 minutes.
Routine check-up and cleaning for existing patients: $149 self-pay, about 45 minutes. We recommend a check-up every 6 months.
Deep cleaning (scaling and root planing) for gum disease: from $220 per quarter of the mouth.

## Fillings
Tooth-colored (composite) fillings: from $160 to $280 per tooth depending on size. Most fillings are done in a single visit of 30–60 minutes.

## Teeth whitening
In-office professional whitening (one 90-minute visit): $499.
Take-home whitening kit with custom trays: $299.
Results usually last 1–3 years depending on diet and habits such as coffee, tea, red wine and smoking.

## Invisalign and clear aligners
Invisalign clear aligners: from $3,800 to $6,200 depending on the complexity of the case. Treatment usually lasts 6–18 months.
The Invisalign consultation, including a 3D scan of your teeth, is free.
Monthly payment plans are available (see payment options).

## Dental implants
A single dental implant including the crown: from $3,900. The full process usually takes 3–6 months because the implant needs time to heal into the bone.
The implant consultation, including a 3D CT scan, costs $99 and the fee is credited back if you go ahead with treatment.

## Crowns and bridges
Porcelain crown: from $1,150 per tooth. We usually need 2 visits about 2 weeks apart.
Bridge: priced per unit, from $1,100 per unit.

## Root canal treatment
Front tooth: from $850. Molar (back tooth): from $1,250. A crown is often recommended after a root canal on a back tooth.

## Tooth extraction
Simple extraction: from $180. Surgical extraction or wisdom tooth removal: from $350 per tooth.

## Children's dentistry
Children's check-up and cleaning: $99 self-pay. We offer fluoride treatment and sealants to prevent cavities. Kids get a small prize after every visit.

## Cosmetic dentistry
Porcelain veneers: from $1,200 per tooth. A free smile consultation is available for veneers and smile makeovers.

## Sedation and nervous patients
We offer nitrous oxide ("laughing gas") for $75 per visit, and oral sedation for longer procedures. Many of our patients are nervous about the dentist; tell us and we will go slowly, explain each step and agree on a stop signal with you.
