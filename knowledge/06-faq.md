# Frequently asked questions

## Does teeth whitening hurt?
Whitening does not hurt, but some people feel temporary tooth sensitivity for 24–48 hours. We use a desensitizing gel to reduce this.

## Do dental implants hurt?
The implant is placed under local anesthetic, so you should not feel pain during the procedure. Most patients describe mild soreness for 2–4 days afterwards, which is managed with regular over-the-counter pain relief. Sedation is available for nervous patients.

## How long does Invisalign take?
Most treatments take 6–18 months. You wear the aligners 20–22 hours a day and switch to a new set every 1–2 weeks. We check progress every 6–8 weeks.

## How often should I get a check-up?
Every 6 months for most people. Patients with gum disease may need a cleaning every 3–4 months.

## Do you see children?
Yes, from age 3. We recommend the first check-up by age 3 or when all baby teeth have come in.

## Are X-rays safe?
We use digital X-rays, which use up to 80% less radiation than traditional film X-rays. We only take X-rays when they are needed. Tell us if you are or might be pregnant.

## Can I get a quote before booking?
We can give typical price ranges, but an exact quote needs an exam because every mouth is different. Invisalign and veneer consultations are free.

## Do you offer second opinions?
Yes. Bring your X-rays and treatment plan from another dentist and we will review them at a regular exam visit.
