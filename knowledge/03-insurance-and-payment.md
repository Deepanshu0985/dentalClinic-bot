# Insurance and payment

## Insurance we accept
We are in-network with Delta Dental (PPO), Cigna Dental (PPO), MetLife, Aetna Dental (PPO), Guardian and United Concordia. We are happy to file claims for most other PPO plans as an out-of-network provider. We do not accept HMO/DHMO plans or Medicaid.
Patients should bring their insurance card to their first visit. Our team can check your benefits before your appointment if you send us your insurance details.

## Payment methods
We accept cash, all major credit and debit cards, HSA and FSA cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay. Payment is due at the time of the visit.

## Payment plans and financing
For treatment over $1,000 we offer financing through CareCredit and Sunbit, with 0% interest plans for 6 or 12 months for approved patients. Invisalign and implants can also be paid with an in-house monthly plan with a down payment.

## Brightsmile Membership Plan (no insurance)
For patients without insurance: $29 per month or $299 per year for adults, $19 per month for children. The plan includes 2 check-ups and cleanings per year, all routine X-rays, one emergency exam per year and 15% off all other treatments.
