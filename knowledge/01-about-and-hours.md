# About Brightsmile Dental

## Who we are
Brightsmile Dental is a family and cosmetic dental clinic in Austin, Texas. We treat children (from age 3), adults and seniors. Our team includes 3 dentists, 4 hygienists and a friendly front-desk team. We have been serving Austin since 2011.

## Location and parking
Address: 2400 Lakeview Drive, Suite 110, Austin, TX 78701.
Free parking is available in front of the building, including 2 accessible parking spots. The clinic is on the ground floor and is fully wheelchair accessible. We are a 5-minute walk from the Lakeview bus stop (routes 7 and 20).

## Opening hours
Monday to Thursday: 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM.
Friday: 8:00 AM – 4:00 PM.
Saturday: 9:00 AM – 1:00 PM (every Saturday).
Sunday: closed.
We are closed on major US public holidays (New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving and Christmas Day).
