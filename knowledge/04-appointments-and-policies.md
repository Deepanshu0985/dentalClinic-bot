# Appointments and policies

## How to book
Patients can request an appointment through the website chat at any time, by calling (512) 555-0147 during opening hours, or by email. Our front desk confirms the exact time by phone or email within one business day. New patients can usually be seen within 1 week.

## First visit
Please arrive 10 minutes early to complete forms, or fill them in online beforehand using the link we send you. Bring a photo ID, your insurance card (if you have one) and a list of the medicines you take.

## Cancellations and rescheduling
Please give us at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule. Missed appointments or cancellations with less than 24 hours' notice may be charged a $50 fee.

## Running late
If you are more than 15 minutes late we may need to reschedule your appointment so that other patients are not kept waiting.

## Children
Children under 18 must be accompanied by a parent or legal guardian at their first visit.

## Privacy
We follow HIPAA rules. Medical information is only shared with your permission. Please do not send detailed medical records through the website chat; our team will collect them securely at your visit.
